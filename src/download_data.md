# Getting the data

1. Go to https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/nsch/data/datasets.html
2. Download the **Stata** topical files for 2024 and 2025:
   - https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/nsch/datasets/2024/nsch_2024_topical_Stata.zip
   - https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/nsch/datasets/2025/nsch_2025_topical_Stata.zip
3. Unzip both into `data/` so you have `data/nsch_2024e_topical.dta` and `data/nsch_2025e_topical.dta`.

The NSCH public-use files are U.S. government works and free to use. They are not redistributed here only because of their size.
